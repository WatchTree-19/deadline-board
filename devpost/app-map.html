The read path

Browserpublic/app.js read() calls /api/read?url=...&country=...
↓
Serverserver.js checks the country, limits reads per visitor, catches errors as plain messages
↓
Fetchlib/read.js readCompetition() fetches the page and, for Devpost, its /rules page through lib/fetchpage.js fetchPage() (public addresses only, 12 s, 4 MB)
↓
Readlib/extract.js readPage(): findDeadline, findEligibility, findFee, findPrize, findRequirements, videoLimit
↓
ShowshowTicket() converts the deadline to your time with Intl.DateTimeFormat; addCurrent() saves to localStorage; renderBoard() sorts; tick() counts down every second

Where to change things

  • A new time zone name: ZONES in lib/extract.js
  • A new country or its other names: COUNTRY_NAMES
  • Words that mark a deadline: DEADLINE_WORDS; headings for what to submit: SUBMIT_HEAD
  • Board columns and statuses: renderBoard() and STATUSES in public/app.js

A practice to reuse

Every answer carries its evidence. When a tool reads documents for you, return the source line with each answer, so a person can trust it at a glance or catch a wrong one.
