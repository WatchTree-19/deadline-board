# Deadline Board

Paste a competition link, get the facts that decide whether you enter, and keep every entry on one board sorted by what closes first.

## The Unique Kernel
It reads the rules page for you and answers the four questions that actually decide an entry (when does it close in my time, can I enter from my country, does it cost anything, what do I hand in), and every answer shows the line on the page it came from, so you can trust it or check it in one glance.

## Who It's For
A solo builder who enters several hackathons and competitions a month from the UK. Today they open each Devpost or festival page, hunt through the rules for the deadline and its time zone, search for "United Kingdom" in the eligibility list, and keep the dates in a spreadsheet or in their head. Deadlines get missed by a time zone.

## The Core Loop
Find a competition, paste its link, read the four answers, add it to the board. Come back to the board to see what closes next and how long is left, and mark each one thinking, building or submitted.

## Inspiration & Identity
An airport departures board: dark, calm, amber monospace times, the next departure at the top, a countdown that ticks. Serious and trustworthy, not playful.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
He enters a lot of competitions for his products and the deadline, time zone and UK eligibility checks are repeated by hand every time.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Paste the Build With AI: Basics link: the app shows "Mon 26 Oct, 21:00 GMT" in UK time (5pm EDT, after the UK clocks go back) with a live countdown, "UK can enter: yes" with the eligibility sentence quoted, "Free: no purchase necessary" quoted, the prize, and the list of what to submit. Add it, add two more, and the board orders them by deadline with live countdowns. The "oh, that's cool" beat: the quoted line under every answer.

## The POC Boundary
- Read one link (and its Devpost rules page when it is a Devpost link).
- Four answers plus prize, each with its source quote, or an honest "not found on the page".
- Your time zone from the browser; the country to check picked once and remembered.
- A board saved in this browser, sorted by deadline, with countdowns and a status per entry.

## Later
Reminders by email or calendar file, pages that need a browser to render, sharing a board, finding competitions instead of pasting them.

## Explicitly Cut
- An AI model to read the page: pattern rules are free, need no key, and can quote exactly where each answer came from.
- Accounts and a server-side database: one person's board fits in the browser.
